Logistics & CourierIndia (Mumbai) Website and Operations Platform for a Courier and Logistics Company

The client is a Mumbai-based courier and logistics company that delivers parcels within India, abroad and locally. They needed a website to win new customers, and one place where their staff could handle customer support, parcel pickups, daily records and GST invoices — all at a low running cost.

  • A public website that presents the company and its delivery services to new customers, with a light and dark mode
  • Separate logins for customers and company employees, where each employee sees only what their role needs. Sign-up is confirmed by email, and a forgotten password is reset with a code sent by email
